## Mailing list

When you subscribe I collect your first name and email address. I use them only to email you about new posts and research updates.

The list is run on [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/legal/privacy/), which stores your details on my behalf and may process them outside the UK. Mailchimp also records basic information such as when you subscribed and whether emails were opened.

I use your details because you asked to subscribe (your consent). Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can unsubscribe at any time. I keep your details until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them.

## This website

This site has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your own browser and never sent to me.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. Both services receive your IP address when your browser fetches the page, as with any website.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details I hold about you by emailing henrinnes@yahoo.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how your details are handled, you can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/).
