Knowledge about how ransomware gangs operate (their tactics, tools and target platforms) is scattered across conference talks, incident-response briefings and security videos. RansKnow collects it into one structured dataset.

## What's in it

* **1,128 video records** (1,034 with full transcripts) from **92 cybersecurity YouTube channels**, including The DFIR Report, SANS, Black Hat, DEF CON, CrowdStrike, Mandiant and Unit 42
* Videos chosen with keyword-based inclusion criteria and filtered for technical depth
* **37 structured features** per video, extracted by a Knowledge Agent pipeline and aligned with MITRE ATT&CK: ransomware families mentioned, tactics, tools such as Cobalt Strike, Mimikatz and Rclone, and platforms (Windows, Linux, ESXi)

## Beyond the data

The repo also includes a full modelling and audit pipeline: baselines, calibration, conformal prediction, out-of-distribution detection, a label-quality audit, and robustness checks. There's a getting-started notebook to explore it quickly.

RansKnow also feeds the knowledge agent in [Agentic SABRE]({% post_url 2025-10-30-agentic-sabre %}).

Find out more by [visiting the project on GitHub](https://github.com/Henrinnes/RansKnow).
